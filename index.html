About Me

I am an assistant researcher at Tsinghua University, working in the Natural Language Processing Lab (THUNLP) on multimodal large language models. I received my Ph.D. degree in June 2024 from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I was a member of LAMP, advised by Prof. Qixiang Ye. I received my B.E. degree from Wuhan University in June 2019.

My research interests are multimodal large models and multimodal data representation learning, specifically high-resolution image visual perception, multimodal video understanding, and remote sensing multimodal large models. Recently I am also interested in generative visual perception for everything in the world, as well as spatial intelligence.

I have published more than 30 papers at top-tier artificial intelligence venues, including CVPR, NeurIPS, ACL, ICCV, ECCV, IJCV, IEEE TPAMI and IEEE TNNLS, with over 2,000 citations on Google Scholar. One of them was selected as a Best Paper Candidate at CVPR 2026. I hold 6 granted national invention patents, and I serve as a reviewer for CVPR, NeurIPS, ECCV, IEEE TPAMI and IEEE TIP, where I received the Outstanding Reviewer Award at CVPR 2026.

I am the first author of LLaVA-UHD (ECCV 2024), a large multimodal model that perceives images in any aspect ratio and high resolution, and a co-first author of its successor LLaVA-UHD v2. Welcome for discussion and collaboration, feel free to drop me an email.

Selected Contributions

  • LLaVA-UHD / LLaVA-UHD v2 — a family of large multimodal models for native any-aspect-ratio, high-resolution image perception, released as open source under thunlp/LLaVA-UHD.
  • Convex-hull Feature Adaptation (CFA) — an approach to oriented and densely packed object detection, published at CVPR 2021 and extended in IEEE TCSVT 2022.

Research

My research centres on multimodal large models and multimodal data representation learning: how visual signals of any resolution, length or modality can be represented so that large models perceive the world faithfully rather than through a coarse summary.

High-Resolution Image Visual Perception

Native-resolution, any-aspect-ratio image encoding and hierarchical feature pyramids that supply fine-grained visual detail to large models instead of forcing images into a fixed, low-resolution square.

LLaVA-UHD (ECCV 2024) · LLaVA-UHD v2 (AAAI 2026) · LLaVA-UHD v3 (ECCV 2026)

Multimodal Video Understanding

Extending multimodal large models from single images to video, where temporal redundancy, long context and reasoning over time all have to be handled together.

Video-R1 (NeurIPS 2025) · FlexiVideo (CVPR 2026)

Remote Sensing Multimodal Large Models

Adapting multimodal large models and agents to remote sensing imagery, which is characterised by kilometre-scale scenes, extremely small targets, and intricate geospatial relations.

GeoViS (CVPR 2026 Oral) · RSMeM (ACL 2026)

Recent Interests

Generative Visual Perception for Everything in the World

Using generative modelling as the interface for perception, so that a single model can describe and reconstruct arbitrary visual concepts rather than a closed set of categories.

Cheers (ECCV 2026)

Spatial Intelligence

Giving multimodal models an understanding of 3D space, geometry and physical layout, as a foundation for reasoning about and acting in the real world.

If any of these directions overlap with your work, I would be glad to hear from you — get in touch.

Earlier Work

Object Detection

Oriented and densely packed object detection via convex-hull feature adaptation, and integral migration of pre-trained transformer encoder-decoders into detectors.

CFA (CVPR 2021, IEEE TCSVT 2022) · Conformer (IEEE TPAMI 2023) · imTED (ICCV 2023)

Weakly Supervised Segmentation

Instance segmentation from a single point per object, using part-based attention and discriminatively matched part tokens to recover the full object extent.

AttentionShift (CVPR 2023) · DMPT (IJCV 2025)
