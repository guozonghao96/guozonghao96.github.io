Biography

I am a researcher working on computer vision and multimodal large language models. I received my Ph.D. degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I was a member of PRISDL in the School of Electronic, Electrical and Communication Engineering, advised by Prof. Qixiang Ye. I received my B.E. degree from Wuhan University, Wuhan, in June 2019.

My research interests include computer vision and deep learning, specifically multimodal large language models, high-resolution visual perception, object detection and instance segmentation. I am the first author of LLaVA-UHD (ECCV 2024), a large multimodal model that perceives images in any aspect ratio and high resolution, and a co-first author of its successor LLaVA-UHD v2.

Selected Contributions

  • LLaVA-UHD / LLaVA-UHD v2 — a family of large multimodal models for native any-aspect-ratio, high-resolution image perception, with open-source code released under thunlp/LLaVA-UHD.
  • Convex-hull Feature Adaptation (CFA) — an approach to oriented and densely packed object detection, published at CVPR 2021 and extended in IEEE TCSVT 2022.
  • Conformer — a dual-branch backbone coupling local convolutional features with global representations for visual recognition and detection, published in IEEE TPAMI 2023.

Research

My work develops visual representations and multimodal architectures that preserve fine-grained detail, ranging from high-resolution multimodal large language models to oriented object detection and weakly supervised segmentation.

Multimodal Large Language Models

Architectures and training recipes that connect visual encoders with large language models for general-purpose visual understanding, including controllable region-level captioning.

LLaVA-UHD (ECCV 2024) · LLaVA-UHD v2 · ControlCap (ECCV 2024)

High-Resolution Visual Perception

Native-resolution, any-aspect-ratio image encoding and hierarchical feature pyramids that supply multi-scale visual detail to language models.

LLaVA-UHD (ECCV 2024) · LLaVA-UHD v2

Object Detection

Oriented and densely packed object detection via convex-hull feature adaptation, and integral migration of pre-trained transformer encoder-decoders into detectors.

CVPR 2021 · TCSVT 2022 · ICCV 2023 · TPAMI 2023

Segmentation and 3D Scene Understanding

Pointly supervised instance segmentation with iteratively estimated part-based attention, and few-shot semantic segmentation of 3D point cloud scenes.

CVPR 2023 · 3DV 2022 (Oral) · AAAI Workshop 2021
